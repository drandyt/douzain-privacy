# Douzain — Request deletion of your data

This page is for players of **Douzain**, published by **Studio Douzain**, who want data
associated with their installation deleted.

## How to request deletion

1. Email **studio.douzain@gmail.com** with the subject line **"Data deletion request"**.
2. Tell us, if you can, the **display name** you used in multiplayer (shown in the app's
   multiplayer setup screen). If you never played multiplayer, just say so — see below for
   what this means for your data.
3. We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within **30 days**.

You can also start a request from the web page **[relay.douzain.com/account/delete](https://relay.douzain.com/account/delete)**, which opens the same email for you. If the app shows you a **Player ID** (in the format `DZ-XXXX`), include it — it lets us find your data reliably. If Douzain accounts are available on your device, you can also delete your account yourself inside the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or with a one-time deletion code on that web page; a deleted account is deactivated at once and permanently erased after a 30-day restore window.

No account exists, so we never hold your name, email, or contact details — the email
address you write from is used only to reply to you and is not linked to your game data.

## What is deleted, what is kept

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Optional gameplay analytics (sessions, match results, progress — only ever collected if you opted in, auto-deleted after 180 days regardless) | **Self-serve in the app**: About → "Delete my analytics data" asks our server to erase everything tied to your device's random install identifier (best-effort, can take a few days; this data is anonymous so we can only locate it by that identifier, not by anything that identifies you). You can also stop collection instantly yourself: About → sharing toggle OFF, which also wipes anything not yet sent. |
| Creature capture registry entries ("Great Ledger" serial numbers) | **Deleted on request**, located via your multiplayer/capture player ID. |
| Online multiplayer match data | **Deletes itself automatically** — live matches ~2 hours after inactivity, play-by-mail matches after 30 days of inactivity. No request needed. |
| Crash reports (Sentry) | Contain no personal information (no names, no location, no identifiers tied to you). Retained per our crash-reporting provider's standard retention and used only to fix bugs. |
| Everything else (camera, location) | **Never collected in the first place** — camera and location are used live on your device and never transmitted, so there is nothing to delete. |

There is no additional retention period after a completed deletion request.

Full details: [Douzain Privacy Policy](https://drandyt.github.io/douzain-privacy/).
