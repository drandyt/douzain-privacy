# Douzain Support

Douzain is a tactical creature game for Android and iPhone, made by **Studio Douzain**.

## Get help

Email **studio.douzain@gmail.com**. Tell us what happened, what phone you have, and roughly when
it happened. We read every message.

## Find your Player ID

Open Douzain, open Settings, then ACCOUNT. Your Player ID is shown there and starts with `DZ-`.
Include it if you write to us about an online match, the ladder or a creature serial number.

## Known issues

- Your collection and progress are stored on your phone. If you delete and reinstall the game,
  or switch phones without a phone backup, that progress is lost. Android backup and iCloud Backup
  may restore it if they are turned on for the app.

## Test builds

Builds you get through a test programme are pre-release. Things can change or break, and test
builds use a test ledger that is deleted before the public launch.
